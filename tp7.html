Ladghar Beach - A Scenic Escape

Introduction

Ladghar Beach, located in the Dapoli region of Maharashtra, is known for its serene beauty, red-hued sands, and peaceful ambiance. It is an ideal getaway for travelers looking to relax amidst nature. The beach is also famous for its dolphin-spotting boat rides.

Visitor Information

Ladghar Beach offers a mix of relaxation and adventure. Visitors can indulge in activities like parasailing, jet skiing, and banana boat rides. The beach is also a great spot for evening walks and enjoying mesmerizing sunsets.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Ladghar Beach is from October to March when the weather is pleasant and ideal for sightseeing and water sports.

How to Reach

By Road: Ladghar Beach is around 250 km from Mumbai and 190 km from Pune. It is well connected via NH-66.

By Train: The nearest railway station is Khed, around 40 km away.

By Air: The nearest airport is Pune Airport, approximately 190 km away.

Nearby Attractions

  • Harnai Beach - Famous for its fish market and scenic beauty.
  • Anjarle Beach - Known for its clean sands and ancient Ganpati temple.
  • Murud Beach - Offers breathtaking views and water sports activities.
