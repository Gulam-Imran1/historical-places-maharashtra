Jaigad Fort

About Jaigad Fort

Jaigad Fort is a coastal fortress located in Maharashtra’s Ratnagiri district. It was built by the Bijapur Sultanate in the 16th century and later captured by the Marathas under Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. The fort served as an important naval base, strategically positioned to guard the Jaigad Creek and the Arabian Sea.

Key Attractions

  • Scenic view of the Arabian Sea
  • Jaigad Lighthouse offering panoramic views
  • Historic Maratha-era fortifications
  • Nearby Ganpatipule Beach

Visitor Information

Location: Ratnagiri District, Maharashtra, India

Timings: 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Entry Fee: Free

Best Time to Visit: October to February

How to Reach: The fort is accessible by road from Ratnagiri city (40 km) and is well connected to Mumbai and Pune.
