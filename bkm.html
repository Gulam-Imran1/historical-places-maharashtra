Bibi Ka Maqbara

Introduction

Bibi Ka Maqbara is a historic monument located in Aurangabad, Maharashtra, India. It was built in the late 17th century by Prince Azam Shah in memory of his mother, Dilras Banu Begum, the wife of Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb. The monument is often called the "Taj of the Deccan" due to its resemblance to the Taj Mahal.

History

Bibi Ka Maqbara was commissioned by Azam Shah in 1660 to honor his mother. The structure was built under the supervision of architect Ata-Ullah and engineer Hanspat Rai. Due to budget constraints imposed by Aurangzeb, the mausoleum was constructed using less expensive materials compared to the Taj Mahal. Despite this, it remains one of the finest examples of Mughal architecture in India.

Architecture

The monument is designed in the Mughal architectural style, featuring a large central dome, four minarets, and intricate floral designs. The structure is primarily made of plaster-coated basalt, with marble used for decorative elements. The main entrance has a beautifully designed gateway leading to a Charbagh-style garden, enhancing the elegance of the tomb.

Significance

Bibi Ka Maqbara is an important historical and architectural landmark in Maharashtra. It symbolizes the grandeur of Mughal architecture and serves as a major tourist attraction in Aurangabad. The site is maintained by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) and continues to attract visitors from around the world.

Visitor Information

Location: Aurangabad, Maharashtra, India

Timings: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM

Entry Fee: ₹25 for Indians, ₹300 for Foreigners

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Things to Do

  • Explore the intricate carvings and architectural beauty of the mausoleum.
  • Visit the museum near the site, which showcases Mughal artifacts.
  • Walk through the lush gardens surrounding the monument.
  • Experience the historical significance of Aurangabad by visiting nearby attractions.

Nearby Attractions

  • Ajanta & Ellora Caves: UNESCO World Heritage rock-cut caves.
  • Daulatabad Fort: A historic fort with stunning architecture.
  • Panchakki: A 17th-century water mill in Aurangabad.
  • Grishneshwar Temple: One of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva.

How to Reach

Bibi Ka Maqbara is well connected by road, rail, and air. The nearest railway station is Aurangabad Railway Station, about 6 km away. The nearest airport is Aurangabad Airport, located approximately 11 km from the monument.
